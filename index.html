Overturn

Founder. An AI agent platform that fights denied health-insurance claims.

Overturn parses X12 835 remittance files and scanned explanation-of-benefits PDFs, drafts a payer-specific appeal with Claude, and blocks any appeal whose citations don't match the policy text word for word, since one made-up citation can sink an appeal.

A Temporal pipeline runs the work behind a Next.js front end, FastAPI workers and Postgres with pgvector. Appeals go to the BCBS portal end to end through a Playwright submitter, with submitters for five more payers. Patient data is encrypted at rest under a business associate agreement with Anthropic, and the workflow came out of on-site discovery at clinics.

Temporal · Next.js · FastAPI · Postgres · Playwright · Claude

Myon

Prototype. An AI strength coach that reads each muscle.

Myon is built for compression wear with textile EMG and motion sensors, and every screen answers one question: what should you do next? The hardware doesn't exist yet, so a physiologically grounded simulation models muscle activation, reps in reserve from rep velocity, fatigue, left/right asymmetry, synergist compensation and multi-day recovery.

It runs as an installable phone app that works offline, with no build step and no dependencies.

SkinCheck

Skin-lesion segmentation and classification for early melanoma detection.

An Attention U-Net segments lesions on the ISIC 2017 data, and a classifier trained on ISIC 2018 flags malignant ones. The data is 80% benign, so I moved from plain binary training to focal loss, oversampling and a 3-fold cross-validation ensemble tuned for F2, which raised sensitivity on malignant lesions from 32% to 96.6%.

PyTorch · Attention U-Net · focal loss · ISIC
